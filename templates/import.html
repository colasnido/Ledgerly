Import bank statement (CSV)

Upload a CSV from your bank. The app will parse it, then ask Claude to suggest a chart-of-accounts category for each transaction. You'll review and confirm before posting.

What CSV columns are recognized?
  • Date: any of date, transaction date, posted date
  • Description: any of description, memo, details, payee
  • Amount: amount column (positive = deposit, negative = withdrawal), OR a debit + credit column pair
